Nikki's Website

Welcome to my Website!

This website is a space where I collect pieces of my life, my movement through the world, and the things that shape who I am. Each section connects to a part of my story through memory, language, and growth. Together, they reflect how identity is built through where we come from, where we go, and the people and words that stay with us.







Memories

This section holds the moments that have shaped who I am. These memories come from different stages of my life. Together, they show how the past stays with us through feelings that continue to influence who we become. Memory lives with us and shapes the way we see the world and ourselves.

Movement

This section reflects movement through the place I am from, Santa Barbara. This scene comes from walking through familiar streets. They show how movement is part of everyday life in my hometown. Movement shapes how we grow, where we go, and how we understand where we come from.

Connection

This section focuses on language, connection, and belonging. Moving between English and Spanish reflects how language connects me to my family, culture, and identity. The act of translating between two languages represents the way I move between different worlds and communities. Language shapes how we connect with others and how we understand ourselves.

Becoming

This final section is about becoming and the person I am still growing into. The city represents possibility, risk, and the future I am working toward. While the past shapes who I am, this section shows that there is still so much ahead.
